# How to play Marion Mystery

Marion Mystery is a social-deduction party game for **4–20 players** — friends on Mac, Windows and iPhone, plus Marion agents (Daniel, Moose, Kanye, Barry, Greek, Quill, Sift, Bot Builder) and any external agent. Everyone is an animated berry.

## Getting in

1. Open the game (from the Marion HQ **Arcade**, or `/?mystery`).
2. Type your name and pick your **view** — **3D Third Person**, **3D First Person** or **2D Simplified**. It's locked once the match starts (the host can also force one view for everyone).
3. **Quick Play vs Marion Bots** (solo, works offline), **Create Room** (share the 6-letter code / QR / invite link) or **Join by Code**.
4. In the **waiting room**, walk around, visit the **wardrobe mirror** to customise your berry, and wait for the host to press **Start**.

## The modes

- **Classic** — bright maps. Crew do tasks and vote out murderers; murderers kill, sabotage and vent. It only goes dark if someone sabotages the **Lights**.
- **Lights Out** — the map is dark. Every crewmate carries a **flashlight** (F) with a draining battery; recharge at glowing stations or grab battery pickups. Your beam reveals others and gives you away. When a killer lurks close, your light **flickers** and your heart races. Murderers see a little in the dark and can **Blackout** a room.
- **Hide & Seek** — the seeker is revealed; everyone else hides. Tasks take time off the clock; in the final countdown the seeker speeds up and gets brief pings of where hiders are.

## Roles

Crew: Crewmate, Sheriff (gun; misfire rules; the gun drops for a Hero when they die), Detective (body clues + footprints), Medic (one shield per round + portable vitals), Engineer (vents + one remote fix), Lookout (portable camera; cams work during comms sabotage), Tracker (tag + arrow), Mayor (double vote; reveal for a third).
Murderers: Murderer (knife, throw, sabotage, vents), Poisoner (delayed kill), Accomplice (sabotage + vents, no kill).
Neutral: Jester (wants to be voted out), Survivor (just stay alive).

## Winning

- **Crew**: finish all tasks, or eliminate every killer.
- **Murderers**: equal or outnumber the crew, or let a critical sabotage run out.
- **Timer** (if set): the host picks murderers / crew / draw.
- **Jester**: gets ejected. **Survivor**: alive at the end (wins with whoever won).

## Controls

| | Keyboard & mouse | Touch (iPhone) | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | WASD / arrows | left-thumb joystick | left stick |
| Look (3D) | mouse (click to lock pointer) | drag on the right side | right stick |
| Use / task | E or Space | **Use** button | A |
| Kill / tag | Q | **Kill** button | X |
| Report / inspect | R | **Report** | B |
| Vent | V | **Vent** | Y |
| Sabotage map | X | **Sabotage** | RB |
| Map | Tab | map icon | LB |
| Flashlight | F | **Light** | LT |
| Shoot (Sheriff/Hero) | button | **Shoot** | RT |
| Emotes | 1–4 | waiting-room buttons | — |

Every task mini-game also works with keyboard only (arrows + Enter/Space), which is how controllers drive it.

## Accessibility

Settings → reduced motion (no shake/flash/flicker), colour-blind-safe team colours, text size, sound captions, Lights Out high-visibility assist, profanity filter, rebindable keys, look sensitivity and invert-Y. Players are always identified by name and berry type, never by colour alone.

## Sound & music

Everything you do makes a sound: buttons, hovers, toggles and sliders, footsteps that change with the floor (tile, wood, carpet, metal, grass, snow, stone), bumping into walls, flashlight clicks and a low-battery buzz, every task interaction, doors, vents, kills, shots, votes and alarms. Sounds from the world are panned and fade with distance. There are 13 music tracks: the main theme, a waiting-room lounge track, a theme for each of the four maps, Lights Out, Hide & Seek with a final-countdown reprise, meetings, results and practice, plus stingers for role reveals, reports, ejections and wins. Settings → Audio has volume sliders and a **jukebox** to preview every track and pick your menu music. Audio stays silent until your first tap, which iOS requires.
